👤 Personal Information

🔐 Change Password

Your administrator reset your password. Enter the temporary password provided to you, then choose a new password to unlock your account.

After a successful password change, you will be signed out automatically for security.

🚨 Alert Thresholds (Admin Only)

Set low-stock limits for each blood group and the near-expiry warning window. A low-stock threshold of 0 disables that group’s alert.

👥 Provision an Account (Admin Only)

Create staff and laboratory accounts here. Public registration creates hospital requester accounts only.

🔑 Promote a Registered Requester (Admin Only)

Promoting an account grants Admin access to your facility. The person must already have a Hospital Requester account and will need to sign in again.

🔒 Reset a User Password (Admin Only)

Reset another account in your facility. A strong temporary password is generated and shown once. The user must replace it at their next sign-in.

Give this password to the user privately. It is not included in the audit log.
